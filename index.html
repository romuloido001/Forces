1. Key ideas

Tap each example to see why it is balanced or unbalanced.

  • A force is a push or pull between objects. Forces always act in pairs.
  • Forces are measured with a force meter (spring scale) in newtons (N).
  • Net force is what is left after adding forces that point in opposite directions.

Balanced: net force = 0 N

No change in motion: at rest or constant velocity.

Unbalanced: net force ≠ 0 N

Speed, direction, or both can change.

2. Force lab: tug the box

Set a push from each side. Watch the net force and the motion. Try to make the box move at a steady speed.

Notice: with balanced forces the box keeps its current motion. It only stops if you make the net force oppose the motion.

3. Classify the forces

Is the net force zero (balanced) or not (unbalanced)?
